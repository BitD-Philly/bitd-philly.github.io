There's a new gang in town.

The Nameless don't know much about them from basic word-on-the-street, and that's concerning. They're offering protection to [Six Towers](/wiki/six-towers) AND [Nightmarket](/wiki/locations#nightmarket) folk, using the backdrop of the existing [Render](/wiki/npcs#render)/[Spider](/wiki/npcs#claire-strangford) conflict AND the fact that things have been pretty rowdy this past year. 

The citizen's loyalty to you in Six Towers is keeping your hold strong, of course: nobody feels the need to pull on new protection when the Nameless have been (sometimes with difficulty) keeping things locked down. Nightmarket is a different matter: their client base seems to be growing over there. And they DO keep trying to stick their nose into Six Towers . . .
